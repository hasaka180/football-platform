# Editorial policy

Not started. The Journal arrives in Phase 3. Principles agreed now:

- **Original writing only.** No scraped or republished articles.
- **Bylines.** Named authors with biographies; publication and update dates.
- **Sourcing.** Every factual claim is sourced, and numbers link to the page they come from.
- **Opinion.** Clearly separated from fact.
- **Corrections.** Published openly, with the change and the date.
- **Transfers.** Shown as confirmed only with an official club announcement.
  Statuses: Confirmed, Reported, Corroborated, Unverified, Denied, Withdrawn.
  No invented probabilities.
- **Images.** Original or properly licensed only (see LICENSING.md).
